HOW IT WORKS

A difference you can hear.

Phase sends a slightly different tone to each ear. Your brain perceives the frequency gap as a gentle rhythmic pulse. The ten faders mix harmonic carrier pairs for a richer sound.

Load an ordered local set and analyse it in one pass. Phase estimates each track's key, maps its loudness envelope, and ducks the binaural layer through exposed quiet passages. Low-confidence keys hold the last reliable carrier. Your files never leave the browser.

Use stereo headphones at a comfortable volume. This is a relaxation and focus tool, not a medical treatment. Do not use while driving.
