## 🛠 What it offers

Binaural Studio features a ten-voice synthesizer meant for precise audio layering. The application works by mixing audio sources in real time. It offers a frequency range from 1 to 40 Hz for binaural beats. You choose your carrier frequency and toggle between sine or triangle wave patterns for the synthesized tones. ## 🎧 Usage Instructions

Put on a pair of stereo headphones before you launch the software. Binaural beats require a left and right channel to function. Start with your volume low to prevent hearing discomfort. 

Once the app opens, you will see a dashboard with three main sections. The top section controls the binaural synthesizer. Use the sliders to adjust the beat frequency and the specific harmonic pairs. You can select different state presets to change the mood of the session quickly while you listen.

The middle section manages your music and audiobooks. Import your files by clicking the folder icon. Drag your files into the primary deck to begin playback. The software automatically scans your files to determine their key and timing. This lets you align the synthesized tones with the rhythm of your music.

The bottom section handles recording. Click the red record button to capture your audio output. The recorder turns all your mixed layers into one high-quality audio file. You can stop or pause this at any time.

## ⚙️ Customizing the Sound

The synthesizer allows for deep control over the audio environment. By adjusting the carrier frequency, you change the base pitch of your binaural experience. The beat frequency determines the gap between the left and right ear signals, which influences the perceived effect of the sound.

You can save your current configuration as a preset. This is useful when you find a specific combination of tones and music that works well. Simply click the save button near the preset menu to store your settings. You can switch between these saved states as you build long audio sequences. 

## 📁 Managing Files

Binaural Studio keeps all your recordings in a single folder. You can change the default destination in the settings tab. The app supports common audio formats like MP3 and WAV. If you have trouble loading a file, ensure it is not locked by another program.

If you produce a long session, the recording process may take a moment to finish when you click Stop. Avoid closing the application until the progress bar reaches completion to ensure your file saves correctly. 

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What if I do not hear the binaural effect?
Ensure you wear stereo headphones and keep the volume at a moderate level. Open-back or closed-back headphones both work as long as they provide clear stereo separation.

Can I use this without headphones?
The binaural effect requires the separation of the left and right ears. Standard computer speakers will not produce the intended effect.

Does this software require an internet connection?
No. Once you download the files, you can use the software offline. This ensures your sessions remain private.

How do I remove the software?
You can remove Binaural Studio through your Windows Settings menu under Apps and Features. Locate the name of the app and select Uninstall.
